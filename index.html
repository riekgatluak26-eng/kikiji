Who We Are

An organization built around children, not around paperwork.

Kikiji National Orphanage Center (KNOC) is a nonpolitical, nonprofit national charity organization registered with the Government of South Sudan.

We provide education, protection, and family-based care to orphans, street children, and vulnerable children across Yei River County and beyond.

Education

School fees, uniforms, and study support for every child in our care.

Protection

Emergency care and case follow-up for neglected and at-risk children.

Family-Based Care

Pioneering the FBC model — kinship care and trained foster parents.

Belonging

A family atmosphere where children are heard and cared for.

Program 04

Family Strengthening

In our Family Strengthening program, our desired results are targeted at a child, family, and the community. The design of our FS services consists of a combination of two approaches: direct family empowerment (DFE) and community empowerment for family empowerment (CFE). Both approaches work towards the shared aim of family empowerment.

While working with families and communities we aim to achieve self-reliance families and sustainability of communities through a range of services such as:

Livelihood support Parenting workshops Psychological counselling Entrepreneurship training Vocational training

Program 05

Safety and Security

A child cannot heal, learn, or grow without first being safe. Every child at Kikiji is protected by a secure home, the basics they need to thrive, and full access to the care they deserve.

  • Safe Housing: Protection through safe housing solutions for orphans, ensuring a secure living environment.
  • Basic Necessities: Provision of essential housing necessities to support the wellbeing of the children.
  • Health Care Services: Comprehensive healthcare, including mental health services, to address the medical needs of vulnerable children.

Talent Identification & Development

It has helped create a message of peace, unity and harmony between the children of different backgrounds and tribes under the orphanage by training together.

What has changed

Bicycle Initiative

Getting to school, safely and on time.

  • Helped our children arrive at school on time and reduced absenteeism.
  • Reduced protection risks faced mainly by our female learners, caused by the long distance to and from school.
  • Reduced the number of harassment incidents toward our girls on the way to school.
  • Those who received bicycles now have full attendance of class and good performance.
School Feeding Program

A meal that makes room for learning.

  • Improved the children's nutrition and overall health.
  • Helped raise attendance across the center.
  • Increased enrolment of children to school.
  • Helped children understand their lessons better and perform well.
Acrobatics & Gymnastics

Talent, healing, and peace in the same room.

This project has helped children from Yei, South Sudan identify and develop their talents in acrobatics and gymnastic skills. The program has united children, healed them of trauma, promoted talent, and engaged idle children in productive activities.

It has helped create a message of peace, unity and harmony between the children of different backgrounds and tribes under the orphanage by training together.

State House · Juba 18 March 2026

Governor Adil pledges enormous support for establishment of reformatory centre through Kikiji National Orphanage Centre leadership

Juba Wednesday, 18th March 2026 CES-GPU

Governor Emmanuel Adil Anthony held a pivotal meeting with Dominic Living Wawa, the founder of the Kikiji National Orphanage, to advance plans for a major new social welfare project.

The meeting focused on the need for the establishment of the Juba Kikiji Rehabilitation Center, a dedicated facility aimed at protecting the city's most vulnerable children and sanctuary for orphans and underprivileged children.

Dominic Living Wawa expressed his deep gratitude to Governor Adil and the Juba County Commissioner for the successful allocation of land for the construction of the facility.

The Pledge

"An amount of SSP 50 Million (Fifty Million South Sudan Pounds) as the state's necessary support for its establishment."

The Governor emphasized that providing for vulnerable populations is a key priority for the state's social development and support to the needy population, especially the orphans across the State.
