Who We Are

An organization built around children, not around paperwork.

Kikiji National Orphanage Center was formed with an executive committee and a clear purpose: to provide education and emergency protection to neglected children in South Sudan.

Our team in Yei works with the children every day. We are not a large institution. We are a small, close group of staff and volunteers who know each child by name, follow their schooling, and sit with them through the difficult days as well as the good ones.

Education

School fees, uniforms, and study support for every child in our care.

Protection

Emergency care and case follow-up for neglected and at-risk children.

Mentorship

Regular guidance from visitors, staff, and community role models.

Belonging

A family atmosphere where children are heard and cared for.

Our Children

Every child here has a name, a story, and a future worth protecting.

These are a few of the children in our care. They came to Kikiji at different ages and different moments in their lives. Today they are students, leaders, and friends to each other.

Since 2021 Head Prefect

Maya

Maya is the head prefect at her school. Leadership has helped her find her voice and build confidence. She is growing academically and spiritually, and she has a clear dream for her life.

"I want to become a doctor to help other people and provide free medication to orphans."

Since 2021 Age 13

Anthony Adide

Anthony is 13 and dreams of becoming a pilot. Since joining Kikiji in 2021 he has been progressing in his studies and in his faith. He is one of the children currently waiting for a sponsor.

"I don't know without Kikiji, where could I be."

Since 2020 Age 13

Data Calvin

Calvin joined Kikiji in 2020. Last year he ranked among the top ten best performing students in Yei River County. His dedication has been an encouragement to the whole team.

Sponsor a Child

Some of our children are still waiting for someone to stand with them.

A sponsor does not need to carry a heavy load. What each child needs most is steady support: school fees, learning materials, food, and someone who believes in them over the long run.

If you would like to sponsor Anthony, Data Calvin, or another child in our care, please reach out. We will share their story with you and explain exactly how your support is used.

Email the team Call 0924 916 124

What sponsorship covers

  • School fees and materials

    Fees, uniforms, books, and stationery for the full school year.

  • Daily meals

    Nutritious food so the child can learn and grow without hunger.

  • Care and follow-up

    Health needs, mentoring, and regular check-ins with staff.

  • A stable place to live

    A safe home within our center or with supported family care.

To begin a sponsorship or ask questions, email us at kikijiorphanage@gmail.com.

10 August 2026

A visit from Eng. Lemi William

We were honored to receive Eng. Lemi William, CEO of Point Investment Company. He spent the day with the children and shared his own story. His words reminded everyone that present circumstances do not define a future.

3 July 2026

Child protection training with UNMISS

Our Executive Director attended child protection training organized by UNMISS in Juba. The session covered how to identify and respond to threats against children, national child safety policies, case reporting, and the Monitoring and Reporting Mechanism.

16 June 2026

Day of the African Child

On the Day of the African Child we reaffirmed that every child in South Sudan has the right to grow up safe, healthy, and free from harm. We also named the hard truth that too many children still suffer because of conflict, displacement, and poverty.
