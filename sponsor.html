What It Covers

Where every shilling and every dollar goes.

Sponsorship is not a vague promise. It is school fees, meals, medicine, and someone who notices when a child is struggling.

School fees & materials

Fees, uniforms, books and stationery for the full school year.

Daily meals

Nutritious food so a child can learn and grow without hunger.

Health & care

Medical attention, counselling, mentoring and regular check-ins.

A safe home

A stable place to live within our centre or with supported family care.

"I want to become a doctor to help other people and provide free medication to orphans." Maya — Head Prefect
